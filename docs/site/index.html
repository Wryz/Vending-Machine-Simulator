How it plays

Pick a spot. Stock it. Watch it sell.

Start with a $250 used machine and grow until drivers and smart machines run your business for you.

1

Find the spot

Tap anywhere in your city to see who passes by, when, and why a spot is good or bad.

2

Stock what sells

Snacks, cold drinks, coffee, hot food, capsule toys. Set your prices and fill the coils.

3

Grow the business

Collect the coins, hire drivers, plan routes and outplay a rival operator across five chapters.

In the game

Your machines, your streets

A bright, low-poly city that follows your real day and night. Customers walk up to buy, and every sale pops on the map.

Why it's different

Built on the real world

Open map data, the real clock and live weather drive every sale.

A real map

Real streets and places from open map data. Start in your own city or one of nine world cities.

Real time and weather

Coffee peaks at 8 am, cold drinks sell out in a heatwave, and nights are cozy.

Customers you can see

Zoom in and watch people walk up to your machine. The sale lands when they buy.

Grow into an empire

Drivers, routes, a warehouse and smart machines, until the business runs itself.

Fair by design

No forced ads, ever. Watch an ad only when you want a reward.

Your save, your phone

No accounts. Your game stays on your device, and it keeps selling while you're away.
